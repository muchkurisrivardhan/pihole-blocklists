# Pi-hole REGEX filters (NOT an adlist — can't be pulled from a URL).
# Paste under Group Management > Domains > Add a new regex filter. One per line.
# Deduped: each domain appears once as (\.|^)domain$  (matches domain + all subdomains).

# --- Broad catch-all (AGGRESSIVE: may false-positive on legit "promo"/"sponsor"
#     subdomains; delete this line if something breaks) ---
(ads?|advert(s|isement)?|banner|sponsor|promo|track(ing)?)[-_.]?(server|serve|click|pixel|beacon)?\.(?:[a-z0-9-]+\.)*[a-z]{2,}
